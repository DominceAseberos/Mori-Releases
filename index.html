Designed for Peace of Mind

Living 60fps Scenery

Dynamic sun and moon orbits that naturally progress with the exact hour of the day, gentle drifting clouds, and handcrafted nature.

Dynamic Lock Screen

Auto-syncs your lock screen wallpaper to the current hour whenever your screen turns off. 0 MB storage footprint, zero ongoing battery drain.

Mori Live Wallpaper

Run Mori as an interactive system Live Wallpaper behind your home and lock screen with silky-smooth frame pacing.

Multi-Layout Freedom

Choose between the classic alphabetical vertical list with scrub rail or modern horizontal dock strips. Tap any preview to test full screen.

Lightweight & Battery Safe

100% native Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Tailored Lite and Balanced graphics presets ensure your battery stays healthy.

1-Tap In-App Updates

Never miss a new feature. Mori automatically checks for latest releases from GitHub and installs updates with one clean tap in Settings.
